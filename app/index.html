⚠ One missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. After the mission Down to Earth, find Lucia at Jason’s house in the morning; when she mentions meditating, follow her to the balcony and accept. Do it before leaving Chapter 1 — it disappears after that chapter, and there is no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

Sources: OutputLag trophy list; KeenGamer trophy guide & platinum roadmap.

Roadmap to the platinum

Built from the official descriptions: story first, one-off feats while you play, side stories, activities, collectibles and money, then 100% completion. Around 200 hours; no online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. Cheats disable trophies and saving while active.
